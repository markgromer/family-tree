# Research Log

A chronological record of what was learned, corrected, rejected, and promoted.

## 2026-09-20 — Davison / Hipps breakthrough

Live family interview with Larry Davison supplied major ancestry anchors:
- mother: **Betty Louise Hipps**
- father: **Edward Davison**, corrected from an initial uncertain "Dave"
- Edward served in the U.S. Navy
- family story says Edward survived a ship sinking/loss
- Larry reported Swedish ancestry somewhere in the line
- family supplied the name **Jackson Lee Jefferson Hipps**

Independent research then found a period Defiance County WWII service-book entry for **Chief Warrant Officer Edward H. Davison** that names:
- father **Walter M. Davison**, Defiance, Ohio
- wife **Betty Hipps**
- regular Navy entry in **1934**
- training at Norfolk, Virginia
- service at **Guadalcanal, Midway, the Solomons, Iwo Jima, Okinawa and Japan**
- American Defense ribbon with Bronze Arrowhead
- Asiatic-Pacific ribbon with **8 battle stars**
- Victory and Good Conduct awards

This is a high-value identity match because the period source independently joins Edward + Betty and names Edward's father.

Secondary genealogy sources identify:
- Walter M. Davison (working dates 1897–1974)
- **Julia (Rothenberger) Davison** as Walter's wife and mother of Madolyn, Robert and Edward
- Betty Louise Hipps as daughter of **Jackson Lee Hipps** and **Jeanette Ovidia "Jennie" Johnson**
- a Seattle Jackson Hipps born 26 Nov 1879 and dying Nov 1970

Evidence controls:
- Julia Rothenberger as Edward's mother remains secondary until census/vital records are obtained.
- "Jefferson" in Jackson Lee Jefferson Hipps is family testimony until a record spells it out.
- reported Swedish ancestry remains unresolved.
- the ship-sinking story remains unresolved; no ship should be assigned to Edward without muster/service evidence.
- a different Ohio Edward Davison killed aboard USS Intrepid in 1944 had wife Marie Maxine and is explicitly **not** this Edward.

Earlier research surfaced **Gary Edward Davison**, reportedly born 4 Apr 1944 in Chattanooga, as a probable child of Edward H. Davison + Betty Louise Hipps. The underlying source could not be cleanly re-established during the repository synchronization, so this connection is preserved at **D grade** rather than discarded or promoted.

### Wild Bill Davison collision
A user-contributed genealogy page suggested Walter M. Davison may have been a sibling of jazz cornetist William Edward "Wild Bill" Davison of Defiance.

This connection is **not accepted**:
- reliable Wild Bill biographies identify his parents as Edward Davison + Anna Kreps Davison
- the user-created genealogy data proposes a conflicting household
- no primary record currently bridges Walter to Wild Bill

Keep Wild Bill Davison as a D-grade lead/conflict only.

## 2026-09 — Initial reconstruction

### Corrections from family testimony
- Macomber is **not** part of Masen's biological maternal ancestry; it came from Kirsten's prior marriage.
- Kirsten's maiden/birth surname is **Davison**.
- Anne's maiden/birth surname is **Hickok**.
- Herrington is a married surname for Anne and is not an ancestral surname in Masen's biological tree.
- Anne's parents are **Edwin James Hickok** and **Linda Shoenmaker**.
- Mark Gromer Sr.'s parents are **John Gromer** and **Norma Gromer**.
- Susan "Sue" McFadden married Larry Davison, later divorced, and later married George Spear. George is family/social grandfather but not a biological ancestor.

### McFadden breakthrough
Annabelle McFadden's obituary:
- born 4 Jan 1925 in Seattle
- died 20 Apr 2025 in Wenatchee at age 100
- married Robert McFadden
- children included Marilyn, Sue, Robert Jr.
- Sue was later listed with George Spear
- Robert was described as flying in the Pacific during WWII
- Robert and Annabelle later moved to Lake Chelan and became orchardists

This strongly matched family testimony and established the correct McFadden household.

### Hickok breakthrough
Exact family information supplied:
- Edwin James Hickok
- born 13 May 1937
- lived in Washington
- ex-wife Linda Shoenmaker
- daughter Anne Hickok

Research then found Edwin connected to the family of **James Moran Hickok Jr. and Dorothy Porter**, including an obituary for Edwin's sister Delores identifying:
- parents: James M. Hickok Jr. + Dorothy Porter
- brother: Edwin J. "Buck" Hickok

Research pushed the line backward through a compiled Hickok genealogy into colonial Connecticut.

### Wild Bill connection
The working reconstruction places both Masen's Hickok branch and James Butler "Wild Bill" Hickok's branch under early Farmington settler **William Hickocks**.

Working split:
- Masen branch → William's son Samuel
- Wild Bill branch → William's son Joseph

Current calculated relationship if all compiled links hold:
**6th cousins, 8 times removed.**

This should remain marked as **compiled-genealogy supported** until the intermediate links are independently verified.

### Gromer breakthrough
John Henry Gromer's obituary matched the family:
- born 30 Mar 1938 in St. Louis
- died 23 Aug 2005
- wife Norma
- six sons including Mark
- parents William and Dorothy Gromer
- approximately 20-year U.S. Navy career
- later barber
- Walt Disney World security / hairstylist work

This moves the direct Gromer line to:
Masen → Mark Jr. → Mark Sr. → John Henry → William Gromer

The next unresolved bridge is **William Gromer → his parents**.

## Rejected / do not reintroduce without evidence

- Treating Macomber as Masen's biological maternal surname line
- Treating Herrington as Anne's maiden name
- Searching for "Edward James Hickok" after family correction to **Edwin James Hickok**
- Attaching Masen to Missouri Gromer families solely because John was born in St. Louis
- Treating every person named Gromer / Hickok in the same state as related without a documented bridge
- Treating the Wenatchee dentist record for Robert A. McFadden Jr., DDS as evidence that Robert McFadden Sr. was a dentist
- Treating the USS Intrepid casualty Edward Davison (wife Marie Maxine) as Larry's father
