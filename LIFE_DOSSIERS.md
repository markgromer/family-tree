# Life Dossiers

This file turns the family tree into researched lives.

The standard is not merely **who begat whom**. For each confirmed ancestor or useful collateral relative, research:

- occupations and employers
- businesses owned
- military service, rank, units, deployments, medals
- education
- homes, farms, land, mortgages, property values
- census household and class/economic clues
- probate / estate
- churches, clubs, unions, civic groups
- newspapers and advertisements
- lawsuits, arrests, criminal cases, bankruptcy — only with strong identity matching
- migration and why the person moved
- hobbies and personality clues
- photographs, yearbooks, signatures, documents
- major historical events that materially touched the person's life

Do not label someone "rich", "poor", "criminal", etc. from weak evidence. Record the underlying facts, then contextualize them against the place and period.

## Edward H. Davison — WWII Navy

**Relationship to Masen:** maternal great-grandfather  
**Evidence:** B for identity, father and wartime service summary; family oral history for ship-loss story

### Known life
- Son of **Walter M. Davison** of Defiance, Ohio.
- Husband of **Betty Hipps**.
- Entered the regular U.S. Navy in **1934**.
- Period Defiance County WWII material identifies him as a **Chief Warrant Officer**.
- Training/service summary includes Norfolk, Virginia and wartime service connected with **Midway, Guadalcanal, the Solomon Islands, Iwo Jima, Okinawa and Japan**.
- Listed decorations include American Defense with Bronze Arrowhead, Asiatic-Pacific with **8 battle stars**, Victory ribbon and Good Conduct Medal.
- Family oral history from Larry Davison says Edward was aboard a ship that sank/was lost and survived.

### Evidence caution
A different Ohio sailor named **Edward Davison** was killed aboard USS *Intrepid* in 1944 and had a different wife, Marie Maxine. That man is **not** Larry's father.

The specific ship in Larry's family story is still unknown. Do not attach Edward to a vessel simply because it was sunk in a campaign where he served.

### Research next
- Full Navy Official Military Personnel File.
- Muster rolls from enlistment in 1934 through discharge/retirement periods.
- Identify every ship/unit assignment.
- Locate the ship-loss episode and determine whether Edward was aboard at the time.
- Full birth/death records.
- Marriage record to Betty Hipps.
- Census household with Walter M. Davison and Julia Rothenberger.

## Economic / class research protocol

For each ancestor, collect the evidence before summarizing class or wealth:

1. occupation over time
2. owned vs rented housing/farm
3. property value and personal estate in census records
4. acreage and land deeds
5. employees / servants / hired hands
6. probate estate
7. taxes
8. business ownership
9. bankruptcy / liens / foreclosure
10. comparison with local households in the same period

Only after that should the narrative use language such as working class, prosperous farmer, substantial landowner, financially distressed, etc.

## Legal / criminal research protocol

Court or criminal findings require unusually strong identity matching:
- full name
- age/date
- place
- spouse/relative corroboration where possible
- source type
- disposition/outcome

Do not turn an arrest into a conviction, or a same-name newspaper item into a family fact.
