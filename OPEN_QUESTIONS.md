# Open Questions

Ordered roughly by research value.

## P0 — highest priority

### Who were William Gromer's parents?
John Henry Gromer's obituary identifies his parents as William and Dorothy Gromer. John was born in St. Louis in 1938.

Why it matters:
- This is the shortest path to identifying Masen's direct Gromer immigrant line.
- There are established Missouri Gromer families with German origins, but **no connection should be assumed** until William is identified.

Need:
- John Henry Gromer's birth record
- 1940 / 1950 census household
- William and Dorothy marriage record
- William obituary / death certificate
- Dorothy maiden name

### Investigate notable ancestors
For each direct ancestor, search:
- military service
- public office
- land ownership
- businesses / trades
- newspaper coverage
- court / probate records
- migration history
- historically significant events

The goal is **not** to manufacture famous ancestors. It is to identify ancestors whose actual lives are unusually well documented or historically consequential.
