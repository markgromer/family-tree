## Wild Bill Hickok relationship — working conclusion

James Butler "Wild Bill" Hickok (1837–1876) appears to descend from **William Hickocks** through William's son **Joseph**, while Masen's line appears to descend through William's son **Samuel**.

If the colonial chain above is correct, the calculated relationship is:

**Masen Gromer ↔ Wild Bill Hickok: 6th cousins, 8 times removed.**

**Status: C / strong working hypothesis.** The common-family structure is well documented in compiled Hickok genealogies, but every generational bridge should still be independently checked before this is labeled fully proven.
